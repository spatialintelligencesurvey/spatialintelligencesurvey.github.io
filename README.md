# Spatial Intelligence Survey

Website for *A Survey of Spatial Intelligence Benchmarks for MLLMs, VLAs, and World Models*.

Published version: **v0.8** (29 October 2026).

[Read the survey](https://spatialintelligencesurvey.github.io/).

This release contains 9 chapters, 3 benchmark summary tables, and 113 bibliography entries. It adds task examples for anomaly detection, law discovery, constrained scene and animation construction, solver generation, and interactive reconstruction, together with evaluation and analysis. The three benchmark tables are unchanged. The Learning and Real-to-Sim sections are temporarily omitted. It retains the original author-provided taxonomy figure (`framework.png`) and the caption “Fig. 1. Overview of the taxonomy.” The page embeds its styles and original figure and requires no runtime service.
